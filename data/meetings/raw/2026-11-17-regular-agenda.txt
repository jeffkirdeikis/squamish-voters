 13.
 NOTICE OF MOTION

 The following notice of motion has been brought forward for consideration by Mayor Easton. Please note that pursuant to District of Squamish Procedure Bylaw 3032, 2024 Council consideration of the motion will take place at the Regular Business Meeting on December 1, 2026, unless otherwise directed by Council.

Notice of Motion for November 17, 2026: 
THAT Council direct staff, through the Chief Administrative Officer, to bring forward for Council’s consideration a revised, draft 2027 Budget in the draft 2027–2036 Financial Plan that:

a. Provides for no increase in the municipal property tax revenue over 2026.

b. Maintains or improves the accessibility, availability, timeliness and quality of resident-facing services compared with 2026;

c. Achieves paragraphs (a) and (b) without:
i. involuntary layoffs or reductions in the regular hours of bargaining-unit employees; or
ii. increasing the use of contracted services to replace or displace work ordinarily performed by bargaining-unit employees;

d. Identifies and incorporates savings and efficiencies through a CAO-led organizational and operating review, informed by appropriate engagement with the Senior Leadership Team, bargaining agents and frontline employees, residents, and focused on management layers and spans of control, administrative overhead, duplicated functions, operating and approval processes, and opportunities to delegate appropriate operational decision-making authority to frontline staff; and

e. Clearly identifies the aggregate effects of the proposed measures on staffing, resident-facing service performance, reserves, capital projects, debt, and fees and charges.
