Squamish starts work on its first urban forest strategy

🏛️ What council decided

Tuesday, November 3 · Swearing in meeting

On November 3, 2026, Mayor Sean Easton and six councillors were sworn in as the new Squamish council, and council then made its first appointments to outside boards and set its 2027 meeting schedule.

  • A provincial court judge swore in Mayor Easton and Councillors Andersen, DesJardins, Ellis, Perreault, Prosko and Veltkamp at the Squamish Valley Golf Club.
  • Council named Mayor Easton, Councillor Ellis and Councillor Andersen to the Squamish-Lillooet Regional District board. Councillors Prosko, Perreault and Veltkamp are the backups. Passed with no one opposed.
  • Council named Councillors Andersen, Prosko and Veltkamp to the Land Back Task Force. Passed with no one opposed.
  • Council set a monthly rotation of acting mayors (the councillor who fills in when the mayor is away) through the end of 2027. Passed with no one opposed.
  • Council approved its 2027 schedule of regular meetings, usually the first and third Monday of the month, with none in August. Passed with no one opposed.
  • Councillor DesJardins was named to the Squamish Library Board, and Councillors Ellis and Perreault to the Community Forest Board. Both passed with no one opposed.
  • In his first speech, Mayor Easton said council should judge itself by results for residents, not by plans finished or effort spent.
  • Each council member invited one guest to speak. Topics included Brennan Park's pool and rink, support for the arts, more public consultation, and the relationship with the Squamish Nation.

2027 council meeting schedule: 2027 regular meetings: January 5 and 19, February 2 and 16, March 2 and 16, April 6 and 20, May 4 and 18, June 1 and 15, July 6 and 20, September 7, October 5 and 19, November 2 and 16, December 7 and 21.

Other things that stood out

  • How much should property taxes go up in 2027? 52% say up by inflation only; 32% say freeze them (no increase).
  • What should Brennan Park add next? 29% say a second sheet of ice; 18% say a second pool.
  • Big yeses: Make the Highway 99 and Cleveland Avenue intersection safer (144 yes, 23 no); Install the defibrillator at the Brennan Park pickleball and tennis courts this year (141 yes, 25 no); Put the modular shelter somewhere other than the park near Junction Park (135 yes, 24 no).
  • Closest call: Add traffic calming measures on Loggers Lane in front of Brennan Park (52 yes, 52 no).
