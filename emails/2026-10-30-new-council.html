Other things that stood out

  • Downtown parking is split: 36% want permits for residents and paid parking for everyone else; 32% want it all to stay free.
  • Supportive housing on Government Road: 47% say build it as planned, 24% say don't build it.
  • Dikes: 68% say keep waiting for grants rather than borrow or raise taxes to go faster.
  • Pickleball: 62% say Squamish has enough courts for now.
  • Big yeses: a safer Highway 99 and Cleveland Avenue intersection (142 yes, 22 no), double lanes south onto the highway at Mamquam Road (118 yes, 26 no), and bringing back the Bear Aware program (111 yes, 24 no).
  • Closest call: a raised crosswalk on Skyline Drive, 68 yes and 71 no.
